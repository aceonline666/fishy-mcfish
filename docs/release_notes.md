# Release notes

Newest first. The "What's new" block of each version is pasted into Play Console as it stands
(at most 500 characters per language).

```
<en-US>

• Livelier nights: seabirds sleep, your wake sparkles, some fish only come out at night, and the Coral Reef's beach glows.
• Easier start: sunny daylight at first, cheap first upgrades, a clearer tutorial.
• Charting progress shows at the top; locked zones glow red.
• Map, goals and Fishpedia in the gear shop too.
• Tanks show your rarest fish.
• 15 fish freshly drawn.
</en-US>
<de-DE>
• Wetter: Sonne, Regen und Sturm – Schnee und Blizzard in der Antarktis. Oder fest im Menü wählen.
• Lebendigere Nächte: Seevögel schlafen, deine Spur funkelt, manche Fische gibt es nur nachts, der Strand am Korallenriff leuchtet.
• Leichterer Start: erst sonniger Tag, günstige erste Upgrades, klareres Tutorial.
• Der Kartierfortschritt steht oben; gesperrte Zonen leuchten rot.
• Karte, Ziele und Fischpedia auch im Angelladen.
• Becken zeigen deine seltensten Fische.
• 15 Fische neu gezeichnet.
</de-DE>
```

### Changes

- **Weather**: random spells of sun, rain and storm, with snow and blizzards in Antarctica; rain
  comes about every second 20-minute day. The menu can fix the weather to one kind.
- **Night**: seabirds sleep, the wake sparkles in the first zone, some species only come out at
  night, and the Coral Reef's beach glows. Jellyfish tentacles wave like an octopus's arms.
- **A gentler start**: a new game starts with ten minutes of sunny daylight; the night and the
  weather come later. The first bigger basket costs 10 coins and the first seller 100. In the gear
  shop's tutorial only the bigger basket can be bought.
- **Charting at a glance**: while the wader is in an uncharted zone, a line at the top shows its
  progress (fish and species), in place of the zone announcement. The bottom info box is gone.
- **Locked zones**: walking into the barrier makes the zones beyond it glow red with a lock,
  instead of a text.
- Pads are 10% smaller with thinner outlines, closer to the painted style and further apart;
  the drones' dock is 20% bigger.
- Gear shop pads wait a moment before taking coins, so walking across one buys nothing.
- The menu, map, goals and Fishpedia buttons are there in the gear shop too.
- Messages and goal cards stay until they can be read; the longest hints are shorter.
- Tanks show their rarest fish first, so whales and other rare catches are always in view.
- Fishpedia pictures fit tall animals (jellyfish, seahorses) inside their cards.
- **New cute-comic sprites** for 15 species: common bottlenose dolphin, Atlantic footballfish,
  great barracuda, lion's mane, moon, blubber and Nomura's jellyfish (painted without eyes),
  yellowfin tuna, reef manta ray, blue whale, smooth stingray, great white shark, blobfish,
  common fangtooth and giant guitarfish.

## 0.7.0 (version code 7)

### What's new (Play Console)

```
<en-US>
• Orcas and great whales now leap out of the water too, with bigger splashes.
• Leaps sound like water instead of a bang.
• Whales, giant squid and other legendary species are too heavy for drones – catch them yourself! The Fishpedia marks them.
• Jellyfish drift upright. The blackdragon's and viperfish's lures glow, the pelican eel's tail light flashes red.
• Darker nights: no more glow around the wader.
• 4 fish freshly drawn: bluefin tuna, humphead wrasse, blacktip reef shark, red lionfish.
</en-US>
<de-DE>
• Orcas und Großwale springen jetzt auch aus dem Wasser, mit größeren Spritzern.
• Sprünge klingen nach Wasser statt nach einem Knall.
• Wale, Riesenkalmare und andere legendäre Arten sind zu schwer für Drohnen – fang sie selbst! Die Fischpedia markiert sie.
• Quallen treiben aufrecht. Drachen- und Viperfisch leuchten, der Pelikanaal blinkt rot.
• Dunklere Nächte: kein Lichtschein um den Angler.
• 4 Fische neu gezeichnet: Roter Thun, Napoleon-Lippfisch, Schwarzspitzen-Riffhai, Rotfeuerfisch.
</de-DE>
```

### Changes

- **Breaching whales**: the orca, humpback, southern right, sperm and blue whale leap like the
  dolphins, higher with their size, staying up longer and leaping more rarely; crashing back in
  throws more spray and a second, wider ripple.
- **Leap sounds** are a soft splash with spray hiss; whales get a longer one with a deep whump.
- **Legendary species are caught by hand only**: drones leave them to the player, and the
  Fishpedia marks them "Too heavy for drones". Every zone keeps species the drones can take.
- Jellyfish and the Portuguese man o' war keep their upright angle whichever way they drift, in
  the sea and in the tanks; the Pacific blackdragon's lure glows.
- **More lights in the deep**: Sloane's viperfish carries a small blue-white light at the tip of
  its long dorsal ray, and the pelican eel's tail organ glows pink and flashes red twice every few
  seconds. Lures on eel-like bodies follow the swimming wave.
- Fish are drawn side-on only; the views from above, which switched hard on turning, are gone.
- At night the wader no longer carries a pool of light; lanterns, windows, tanks and the headlamp
  light the night.
- **New cute-comic sprites** for Atlantic bluefin tuna, humphead wrasse, blacktip reef shark and
  red lionfish.

## 0.5.0 (version code 6)

### What's new (Play Console)

```
<en-US>
• Day and night: a 20-minute cycle with golden dusks, moonlit nights, lanterns and glowing tanks. Choose the cycle in the menu, or keep it always day or always night.
• The sun and the moon now shine on the water.
• Landscape mode: turn your device, or fix portrait or landscape in the menu.
• Dolphins swim in pods and leap out of the water.
• Sea giants are true to scale – a blue whale dwarfs a great white.
• Fishpedia sorted by location, with a size comparison.
• 8 fish freshly drawn.
</en-US>
<de-DE>
• Tag und Nacht: ein 20-Minuten-Zyklus mit goldener Dämmerung, Mondnächten, Laternen und leuchtenden Becken. Wähle den Zyklus im Menü oder bleib immer bei Tag oder Nacht.
• Sonne und Mond spiegeln sich im Wasser.
• Querformat: Gerät drehen oder Hoch- bzw. Querformat im Menü festlegen.
• Delfine schwimmen in Schulen und springen aus dem Wasser.
• Meeresriesen in echter Größe – ein Blauwal lässt den Weißen Hai winzig wirken.
• Fishpedia nach Fundort, mit Größenvergleich.
• 8 Fische neu gezeichnet.
</de-DE>
```

### Changes

- **Day and night**: a 20-minute cycle (default), real time, always day or always night, chosen
  in the menu. Gold dusk, moonlit blue night, pink dawn; the pier's and gear shop's lanterns, the
  tank and the booths' display tanks light up, and the wader carries a soft light.
- **Sun and moon on the water**: a rippled reflection with a halo and glints that follows the
  time of day.
- **Landscape mode**: the screen turns with the device, or is held in portrait or landscape from
  the menu; HUD, basket bar, spot, shop and panels adapt.
- **Dolphins** show up more often, come in pods of 3–5 and leap out of the water now and then
  (they can't be netted mid-air).
- **Giants true to scale** in the open sea; they turn back earlier and stay visible while they
  stick into view.
- **Fishpedia** grouped by location, ordered by zone and size, with each species shown next to
  a person; lengths above a meter in meters.
- The glowing eyes in the deep appear more rarely.
- **New cute-comic sprites** for European sprat, Atlantic herring, lesser sand eel, Atlantic
  mackerel, Atlantic wolffish, European conger, ocellaris clownfish and blue-green chromis.
- Fixes: Metriorhynchus has its tail again; silvery bellies and pale fins are no longer cut out
  of sprites.
