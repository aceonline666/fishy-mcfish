North Sea beach on the layout grid

Top-down, to scale. One cell is 240 ground units, the width of the pier's deck. Columns count east from the beach's west end; rows count north from the waterline, so the beach has negative rows and the sea positive ones.

Built as shown. Cells are written column,row (x,y), as the in-game grid overlay labels them. The beach is 9 columns wide.
Pier runs in column 3 from the water up to the next "Hire seller" pad: 3,−8…−1 in a new game, one row longer per hired seller, up to 3,−17 with all ten. "Put in tank" stands on it at 3,−3, east of the tank; rows −6…−4 are free of pads. It is the painted pier cut into the steps, a section that repeats once per row, and the lantern end (tools/cut_boardwalk.py).
Booths stand in a line up the beach a column east of the pier, one cell each: the player's at 4,−7, seller n at 4,−7−n (up to 4,−17). Counters face east. The pads are on the west side, on the pier: "Sell fish" at 3,−7, "Hire seller" in front of the next free booth (3,−8 at the start). The booth line is solid.
Buyers come in from the east and queue east of the booths, running up the beach from the player's booth. Columns 4–8, rows −18…−5 (the booth line and the buyers' side, down to the gear shop) cannot be walked on.
Walkable beach reaches up to the row of the next "Hire seller" pad: row −8 in a new game, one row more per hired seller, row −17 with all ten. West of the pier, columns 0–2, rows −18…−3 (tank and belt) are blocked too: there only the two rows along the water are open. From row −5 up, only the pier can be walked on. An invisible wall along the pier's east side (all of column 4, rows −4…−1) keeps the wader from jumping down: it leaves the pier only into the water.
Tank starts as 1 × 1 at 2,−3, two rows off the waterline, and grows west, then up the beach: 1×1, 2×1, 2×2, 2×3, 3×3, 3×4, 3×5 (cols 0–2, rows −7…−3 at the last level). It is solid. "Put in tank" is at 3,−3, on the pier.
Conveyor belt runs in column 2 from the tank's back to the last booth's row: a bigger tank makes it shorter, every hired seller one cell longer. The next fish to be sold is at its north end.
Gear shop covers 3 × 2 cells, columns 6–8, rows −4…−3, south of the booth line so it does not hide the first booths. The drones' dock lies in front of the tank at 2,−1.5, half a cell off the tank and off the water, and only shows once the beach has a drone.
Sea zones sit on the grid: each is 4 rows (960 units) deep, so zone 1 covers rows 0–3, zone 2 rows 4–7, down to zone 6 at rows 20–23.
